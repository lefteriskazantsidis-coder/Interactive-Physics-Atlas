An interactive atlas of ~90 fundamental physical constants, grouped into six
domains and linked by their physical relations. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom,
click a star to see its value in SI, atomic and eV units.

**Live:** https://lefteriskazantsidis-coder.github.io/Interactive-Physics-Atlas

**Built with:** JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas, SVG.
Prototyped with AI design tools; layout algorithm and relation graph are my own.
